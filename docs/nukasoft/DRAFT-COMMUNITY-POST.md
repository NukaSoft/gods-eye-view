## LinkedIn (Pierre voice)

Hook: What if your Dynamics work lived on a real globe, not another grid?

We forked God's Eye View (open MIT globe: traffic, cams, the living map) into a NukaSoft build aimed at Microsoft Dynamics and Power Platform.

Working name: **God's Eye View - Dynamics**.

Idea is simple. Put GPS-located business data from Dataverse next to the real world. Accounts. Service locations. Assets. Work orders. Field resources. Same view a dispatch center already wishes it had, without a black-box price tag.

Time to value is the point. If the community helps shape it, the source stays open. Fork it. PR it. Use it.

Repo: https://github.com/NukaSoft/gods-eye-view

This is early. Demo-safe. Built in public on purpose.

If you live in Dynamics Field Service or Power Platform and care about "where is the work," come look. Tell us what breaks for real dispatch.

Have a super day.
